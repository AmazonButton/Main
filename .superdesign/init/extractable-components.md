# Extractable DraftComponents

These components represent discrete, high-value visual patterns that can be independently extracted, customized, and placed onto canvas designs.

---

## 1. `DeviceTactileCard`
A high-converting IoT card with product thumbnail, real-time online status badge, battery level indicator, and a tactile click target that triggers haptic feedback.

## 2. `AirPodsProvisioningModal`
An authentic Apple-style bottom-sheet modal orchestrating Bluetooth discovery, instant Wi-Fi network saving, and catalog product mapping.

## 3. `OrderCelebrationModal`
A conversion-focused modal featuring celebratory confetti, rising acoustic chime, product summary breakdown, and an animated 60-second cancellation safety bar.

## 4. `MerchantLiveOrderTicker`
A high-priority incoming order banner with audio notification, status progress tracker, and one-click dispatch button.
